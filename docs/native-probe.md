# Deferred native Apple probe

Native development is deferred until the current web prototype's iPhone/iPad provider results are returned. Milestone 1.6 introduces no SwiftUI or tvOS project. Its immediate checklist is in [launch research](provider-launch-research.md).

If native development is later authorized, the smallest probe can be one shared list of genuinely sourced Big 6 title URLs, with iPhone/iPad and tvOS targets. It needs no catalog, provider login storage, backend, or paid API. The scope is Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Prime Video, Paramount+, and Peacock; Max is excluded.

Observe **button → correct native provider → exact expected title** on physical hardware, rather than trusting an accepted-open callback. Track OS/app versions, region/entitlement, movie versus series routes, cold/warm app state, and login/profile continuation. Autoplay is a separate higher capability. A provider Home page, browser, or companion-phone handoff does not pass exact native routing for Apple TV.

Use platform-supported APIs and only provider-documented or physically verified URLs. Keep unsupported routes out of that platform's catalog. A web/iPhone success does not establish Apple TV support. This proposal remains a later milestone, not work to begin after the current deployment.
